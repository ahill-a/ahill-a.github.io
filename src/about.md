I am a Chicago-based game designer and artist currently pursing an
MFA in game design at DePaul University. I received my BFA in game
design from the Universtity of Wisconsin-Stout in 2022, and worked as
a theater tech for a few years before going back to school. 

I've worked on several student and independent jam games over the last
few years. As an artist, I primarily focus on 3D hard surface modeling
or illustrated sprite and UI elements. As a designer, I focus on player
mechanics. More specifically, the user experience and how well
they mesh with the game as a whole (i.e. its narrative and purpose.) 
I am comfortable with the game engine pipeline and basic C# programming 
and am willing to learn more! 

In my free time, I like to experiment with different art mediums.
Currently, I'm getting back into screen printing and find the process
very rewarding. My favorite game genres are adventure and puzzle, though
I like browsing game platforms for off-beat narrative games as well.
