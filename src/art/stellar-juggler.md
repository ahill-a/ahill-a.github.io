For this project, I had to first design a character with a theme and a job, and then create a character sheet with a rendered full-body front, a full-body back, and six expressions. My character is a member of a juggling troupe with a starry theme. 

<img src="/images/art/juggler/juggler_outfits.jpg" alt="Three different outfit concepts">

Her clothing is based on Indian cultural clothing, particularly women's dhoti suits and salwar kameez. Above are three clothing explorations. The silhouettes were based on real life reference with additional original design on top. The design I chose has star motifs scattered around the outfit and a purple and pink color scheme to refelct both the mystery of outer space and bright performer's clothes. She has an astrolabe belt charm and the phases of the moon as cutouts on her scarf.

<img src="/images/art/juggler/baton_concepts.jpg" alt="Nine different juggling baton concepts">

For her juggling batons, I found this really neat website from the [Museum of Juggling History](https://historicaljugglingprops.com/) which has galleries of historical juggling props. My concepts explore different textures and sizes. I landed on a bell shape that provides a baton-like silhouette while continuing the star motif in the openings of the smaller bells attached. Additionally, the metallic material of the bell allows the golden accent color to carry over to the baton.
