The brief for this project was to design a fictional creature by taking traits from existing animals and combining them into a new animal. The goal of the final drawing was to draw this creature in perspective from a front 3/4 view and a back 3/4 view. My final design is the Maned Wolkapi: a creature that is a mix of the maned wolf and the okapi.

<a href="/images/art/wolkapi/animal_studies.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="/images/art/wolkapi/animal_studies.jpg" alt="Various animal studies"></a>

THe first thing I did for this project was to do a few studies of real animals to get used to drawing different forms. It was at this step that I landed on the okapi as one of the animals I wanted to incorporate into my final creature.

<a href="/images/art/wolkapi/creature_concepts.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="/images/art/wolkapi/creature_concepts.jpg" alt="Nine concepts for blended creatures"></a>

After doing some studies, I moved on to creating concepts of different creatures. Each one is created by combining the traits of at least two animals. I chose the first concept in this image to turn into a perspective drawing. This process involved using the side-view image as scaffolding to create volume for the perspective drawing.
