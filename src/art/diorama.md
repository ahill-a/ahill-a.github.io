A dining room diorama I created for my hard surfaces modeling class. There were a couple restrictions for this project in the way of certain pieces we had to include (a table, cutlery) but otherwise we were free to design the diorama as we liked. For my dining room, I was inspired by late 50s and early 60s kitchen design, namely the color sheme and shape language. I consulted vintage advertisements and preserved examples to build a reference library, but unfortunately I have lost those files so I can't include them here.

<a href="/images/art/diorama/diorama_top.jpg" taret="_blank"><img src="/images/art/diorama/diorama_top.jpg" alt="Top view of the entire diorama."></a>

For this project I wanted to try a less realistic style. I kept the base colors simple and played around with different filters in Substance Painter to break up the color.

<a href="/images/art/diorama/diorama_counter.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="/images/art/diorama/diorama_counter.jpg" alt="Kitchen counter isolated from environment showcasing the rounded end and curved shape."></a>

The kitchen counter is one continuous piece. I noticed a lot of counters from the reference I was using had a curved end, which was appealing to me.

<a href="/images/art/diorama/diorama_cutlery.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="/images/art/diorama/diorama_cutlery.jpg" alt="Cutlery showcase displaying a plate, plastic cup, knife, fork, spoon, and cleaver."></a>

Table Settings.

The furniture used in the diorama set. I kept most pieces within a pink/blue/yellow scheme, with the exception of the tv, which I decided to give a wood veneer look to.
