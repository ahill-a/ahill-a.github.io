The Whalebot was a model created and designed for my bachelor capstone project "Duolbots." Duolbots is a multiplayer vehicle fighting game where you choose various weapons and utilites to attach to a vehicle of your choice. As a group, we decided to base all of our vehicles on animals, and I landed on a whale for my design. The goal was to create something playful and toy-like. 

<a href="/images/art/whalebot/whalebot_bottom.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="/images/art/whalebot/whalebot_bottom.jpg" alt="Top and bottom of Whalebot."></a>
<a href="/images/art/whalebot/whalebot_mesh.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="/images/art/whalebot/whalebot_mesh.jpg" alt="Whalebot Wireframe"></a>

Whalebot mesh. The body is a single mesh and the wheels are separate.

<a href="/images/art/whalebot/whalebot_textures.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="/images/art/whalebot/whalebot_textures.jpg" alt="Whalebot albedo, metalic, and roughness maps."></a>

Whalebot has two materials, one for the body and one for the wheels. This project did not utilize normal maps. The texture is a mix of hand painting and noise generators.

<a href="/images/art/whalebot/concept_01.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="/images/art/whalebot/concept_01.jpg" alt="Early Whalebot concept art with a more boatlike form."></a>

The first concept for Whalebot had a much more sailboat-like design. It's here that the idea for attachment points for add-ons was first introduced.

<a href="/images/art/whalebot/concept_02.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="/images/art/whalebot/concept_02.jpg" alt="Silhouette concepts for Whalebot."></a>

I developed Whalebot further to look less reminiscent of a real vehicle. I iterated on the shape of Whalebot to strike a balance between blocky silhouette and visual interest.
