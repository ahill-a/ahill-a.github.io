<h2>{{title}}</h2>

<img src="/images/art/vanship/vanship.jpg" alt="A vanship, a flying vehicle from the show Last Exile.">

I created this model for my hard surface modeling class. The objective for this project was to model and texture aa vehicle from a reference vehicle. I chose to model a vanship from the anime series Last Exile, specifically the vanship belonging to the character Tatiana due to its distinct color scheme. 

<img src="/images/art/vanship/vanship_mesh_01.jpg" alt="Vanship wireframe front and back.">
<img src="/images/art/vanship/vanship_mesh_02.jpg" alt="Vanship wireframe top and bottom.">

For reference I used official concept art by Makoto Kobayashi and images of the Hasegawa 1/72 scale model kit.

<img src="/images/art/vanship/texture_body.jpg" alt="Albedo, roughness, metallic, and normal maps for the main body.">
<img src="/images/art/vanship/texture_pipe.jpg" alt="Albedo, roughness, metallic, and normal maps for the under-body pipes.">
<img src="/images/art/vanship/texture_wing.jpg" alt="Albedo, roughness, metallic, and normal maps for the wings.">

The texturing was split across three materials; one for the main body, the underpipe, and the wings (which are mirrored).
Most of the base color was made with built-in textures from Substance Painter with partially hand painted masks for wear and entirely hand painted rust. 

<img src="/images/art/vanship/closeup_pipe.jpg" alt="Closeup of the back pipe, showing the connectors.">
<img src="/images/art/vanship/closeup_wheel.jpg" alt="Closeup of the wheel well with the graphic decal.">
<img src="/images/art/vanship/closeup_wing.jpg" alt="Closeup of the back of a wing with text saying 'no step.'">
<img src="/images/art/vanship/closeup_front.jpg" alt="Closeup of the front bottom structure with a decal and rust detail.">

The graphic on the vanship is official art edited by me. I found an image of the crest from a fan wiki and painted over it to
produce a high resolution image, as only very small size images were available online.

### Software Used

- Adobe Photoshop
