Get Inked! was created in about 10 weeks for my Game Design Workshop class at Depaul alongside Peri Glick and Aryan Shrivastava. We were tasked with creating a game with one minute of engaging play.

Get Inked! is a frantic tattoo drawing game against the clock. For each tattoo client, players are given a random image with a random color. The player must choose the correct color and trace the image in the play area. To maximize score, the player must balance accuracy of drawing/color with tattooing quickly to complete more client requests.

<img src="/images/games/getinked_02.png" alt="Get Inked! gamplay">

## My roles:
### Gameplay Design
I helped refine the core mechanic of tattoo drawing, primarily tattoo complexity and incentivising the player to balance accuracy and speed. I researched American tattoos from a variety of eras to draw motifs from.

### Art
I created swappable 3D character components to create randomized NPCs during gameplay. I also designed the tattoos and the play field (the frames around each area on screen).
