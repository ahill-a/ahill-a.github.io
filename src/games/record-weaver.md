RecordWeaver is a solo RPG and craft experiment created for my Art Games course. In this game, I experiment with artifact creation to leave a physical record of play. An introspective game meant to be played long term, prompts and a corresponding pattern are chosen with two D4 rolls. The yarn you pick signifies your answer, a personal meaning known only to you.

Early ideation for this game was based on earlier precedents of weaving used in conjunction with data storage; for example, core rope memory used by NASA and historic practices of name drafting where a name is used to form the basis of a loom pattern. Experiments were done in translating the over/under property of weaving into binary 0 or 1 and processing data that way

The gameplay itself draws from "Mystery Knit-Along" projects, where a designer releases a pattern in pieces through clues or choose-your-own-adventure prompts. 

## My Roles
### Game Design
Mechanics and overall gameplay design.

Early versions of this game had a more technical aspect to it with players encoding data in a more literal sense, with responses translated to binary patterns. After collecting player responses during playtesting, I decided a more metaphorical approach was appropriate for the reflective experience I wanted to create.

The current state of the game is also reflective of the DIY ethos I was working under. The game can be played with any extra yarn or strips of fabric the player has on hand, and works with the most minimal of loom setups. 

### Art
Game rules and information was laid out in Affinity Designer. I made my own loom using a picture frame and some nails and wove a demo project one afternoon.
